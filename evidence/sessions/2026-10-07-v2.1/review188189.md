Repair must distinguish known tariffs (explicit zero included) from absent ones, preserve conservative cost or expose incomplete/unpriced cache cost, and propagate uncertainty to store/recompute/SSE. Test missing read and creation tariffs, alongside explicit zero.

2. **P2 — Reopening a session assigns prior session cost to the next turn.** `apps/web/src/components/ConversationView.tsx` resets serverCost.current=0 on session selection; onUsage computes turn deltaCost as delta.summary.costUsd minus that baseline. `apps/local-server/src/server.ts:streamEvents` starts with ping and subscribes to future after_model events; it sends no initial usage baseline. Cumulative UsageBar remains correct. Provide an initial server baseline or a priced per-call cost, and test session switch/reconnect with prior usage.
